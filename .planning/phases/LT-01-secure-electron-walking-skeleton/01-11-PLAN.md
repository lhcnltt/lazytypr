## STRIDE Threat Register — ASVS L2

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-LT01-11-S | Spoofing | renderer/session/native identity | critical | mitigate | Exact sender/role/session/target identity negative tests across alternate paths. |
| T-LT01-11-T | Tampering | schemas, protocol, evidence | high | mitigate | Strict parsers, bounds/unknown-key tests, and fail-closed evidence validation. |
| T-LT01-11-R | Repudiation | findings and outcomes | medium | mitigate | Sanitized command/commit labels, counts, durations, and explicit dispositions. |
| T-LT01-11-I | Information Disclosure | UI/log/report/test output | high | mitigate | Redaction allowlists and scans reject screenshots, audio, content, paths, secrets, and session IDs. |
| T-LT01-11-D | Denial of Service | repeated sessions/input floods | high | mitigate | Bounds, timeouts, exact 20-cycle cleanup assertions, and one-shot runners. |
| T-LT01-11-E | Elevation of Privilege | renderer/network/native bypass | critical | mitigate | Sandbox/IPC/network/native-protocol negative tests cover alternate authority paths. |
