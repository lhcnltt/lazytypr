<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| source/lockfile to compliance evidence | Generated review, provenance, notices, REUSE annotations, and SBOM must represent admitted inputs exactly. |
| third-party source to local adaptation | Copied or substantially adapted native code retains upstream identity and license obligations. |

## STRIDE Threat Register — ASVS L2

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-LT01-SC | Tampering | dependencies and adapted source | high | mitigate | Exact lock review, package approval evidence, integrity comparison, REUSE, immutable provenance, notices, and SPDX SBOM. |
| T-LT01-10-S | Spoofing | package/upstream identity | high | mitigate | Exact registry/repository/version/integrity and pinned upstream revision/path checks. |
| T-LT01-10-T | Tampering | provenance, notices, SBOM | high | mitigate | Cross-artifact tests fail on drift, missing records, or mismatched package/source identities. |
| T-LT01-10-R | Repudiation | compliance decisions | medium | mitigate | Dependency review preserves approval, lifecycle, license, source, and disposition evidence. |
| T-LT01-10-I | Information Disclosure | generated evidence | high | mitigate | Repository-relative metadata and scans reject paths, host identity, secrets, sessions, and user content. |
| T-LT01-10-D | Denial of Service | generated compliance gate | medium | mitigate | One-shot offline commands and parse failures block immediately. |

Any critical or high finding blocks completion; every medium finding is mitigated under ASVS L2.
</threat_model>
