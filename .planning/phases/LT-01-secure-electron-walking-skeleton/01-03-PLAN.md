<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| renderer source to Electron runtime | Packaged HTML/config must not create network or privilege channels. |
| test/runtime output to evidence files | Reports and run sheets could disclose controlled or OS-sensitive values. |

## STRIDE Threat Register — ASVS L2

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-LT01-03-S | Spoofing | evidence platform label | high | mitigate | Exact platform/evidence-level enums and explicit rejection of simulated labels. |
| T-LT01-03-T | Tampering | renderer and evidence configuration | medium | mitigate | Fixed committed entries, exact schema, unknown-key rejection, and clean build/test verification. |
| T-LT01-03-R | Repudiation | evidence metadata | medium | mitigate | Reviewer/timestamp/scenario metadata is required without admitting sensitive identity. |
| T-LT01-03-I | Information Disclosure | tests, traces, run sheet | high | mitigate | Allowlisted outcome fields, privacy tests, and screenshots/video disabled. |
| T-LT01-03-D | Denial of Service | Electron/check runners | medium | mitigate | One worker, bounded timeouts, fail-closed parse errors, and no watch mode. |
| T-LT01-03-E | Elevation of Privilege | local renderer entries | high | mitigate | Packaged-local HTML only, no executable remote/inline assets, and no exposed Electron authority. |

Any critical or high finding blocks completion; every medium finding is mitigated under ASVS L2.
</threat_model>
