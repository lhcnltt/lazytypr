<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| event/timer callbacks to controller | Stale or crafted asynchronous completions may attempt to mutate a newer session. |
| main controller to OS ports | Clipboard and target/paste effects cross into irreversible OS state. |
| main to native helper protocol | Sensitive target identity crosses a bounded local process boundary and must not be logged or persisted. |

## STRIDE Threat Register — ASVS L2

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-LT01-SC | Tampering | imported dependencies | high | mitigate | Use only Plan 01-02's reviewed exact lockfile; no new package installation. |
| T-LT01-04-S | Spoofing | session callbacks | high | mitigate | Main-generated UUID plus current-session and AbortSignal checks before every transition. |
| T-LT01-04-T | Tampering | state/output ordering | high | mitigate | Explicit transition table, synchronous commit flag, at-most-once terminal finalizer, and misuse tests. |
| T-LT01-04-R | Repudiation | output outcome | medium | mitigate | Finite sanitized outcome codes distinguish copied, pasted, copy-only, cancelled, and failed without content. |
| T-LT01-04-I | Information Disclosure | target/result/session data | high | mitigate | Main-only private fields, redacted snapshots, bounded helper protocol, no content logs or reporter values. |
| T-LT01-04-D | Denial of Service | stuck session/timers | high | mitigate | Abortable work, fake-clock two-second bound, idempotent centralized cleanup, stale timer rejection. |
| T-LT01-04-E | Elevation of Privilege | OS capability ports | high | mitigate | Ports are constructed by main only and never exported to renderers; native requests accept finite operations only. |

Any critical or high finding blocks plan completion; medium findings require mitigation under ASVS L2.
</threat_model>
